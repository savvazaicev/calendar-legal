What you get

No subscriptions, no onboarding maze, no server to log into.

Four living views

Month, week, day and agenda — each tuned for a different job, all from the same data.

Google Calendar sync

Two-way sync with an on-device cache. Create and edit events right in the app.

Real recurrence

A built-in RRule engine expands recurring and all-day events the way Google does.

ICS import & export

Bring events in or take them out with standard ICS files, no lock-in.

Find anything

Fast search across every calendar, with per-calendar filters and colors.

Private by design

No ads, no accounts of its own. Tokens are stored AES-encrypted on-device.

Featherweight

A pure 2D app — no VR scene to load, nothing to wear off.

Light & dark

Follows the system theme. The same UISet look as Horizon itself.

How it works

Three steps, ten seconds.

01

Sign in with Google

One tap on the Google consent screen. Calendar, email and name — that is all it asks.

02

Pick a view

Month for the big picture, week and day for the detail, agenda for a plain list.

03

Create & sync

Add or edit events; they write straight back to Google and the local cache stays fresh.

Your calendar stays yours

Calendar data is cached locally on the headset and tokens live in an AES-encrypted store — there is no server of ours to breach. The only data that leaves the device goes to Google, the account you signed into, under the authorization you grant. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
